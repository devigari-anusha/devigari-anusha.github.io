MPLAD Sentinel

AI-Powered Monitoring and Risk Detection System

The problem

Monitoring development projects across many locations involves large volumes of financial and progress records. Manual audits can make it hard to spot suspicious cost variations, duplicate works, delays, and mismatches between spending and physical progress early.

The proposed solution

A monitoring platform that compares sanctioned amounts, expenditure, locations, timelines, and completion milestones to flag possible anomalies in the Members of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS), so officials can prioritize cases for human verification.

MPLAD Sentinel: case study

Overview

MPLAD Sentinel is a proposed monitoring and risk-detection platform intended to help identify potential anomalies, fraud indicators, and inefficiencies in the implementation of MPLADS. It is designed to analyze sanctioned amounts, actual expenditure, project locations, timelines, and physical completion milestones.

What it proposes to do

  • Identify unusual project costs by comparing expenditure against baseline rates.
  • Flag potentially duplicated works using text similarity and geographic proximity.
  • Detect delays and discrepancies between funds spent and reported physical progress.
  • Assign explainable High, Medium, and Low risk classifications.
  • Present project information through centralized dashboards and GIS maps.
  • Help district and government officials prioritize cases for human verification.

Proposed workflow

  1. Collect sanction, expenditure, location, and milestone data.
  2. Clean and validate the incoming records.
  3. Detect cost outliers, possible duplicates, and progress discrepancies.
  4. Generate explainable risk alerts.
  5. Display results on a dashboard with map views.
  6. Authorized officials verify flagged cases and decide on action.

Multi-dimensional verification

Compares financial records, physical progress, and geographic information rather than relying on a single data source.

Explainable alerts

Gives an understandable reason for each risk flag so officials can investigate the underlying issue.
